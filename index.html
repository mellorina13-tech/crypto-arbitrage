About the Scanner

The engine pulls the full USDT order-book ticker set from 8 exchanges (one bulk request each) and computes arbitrage from executable prices only: buy at the lowest ask, sell at the highest bid. Corrupted quotes deviating more than 35% from the market median are rejected. Net profit accounts for both taker trading fees and the recommended blockchain transfer fee for your capital size.
